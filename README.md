## GoldenDog Linux Support

To open a support ticket click on [issues](https://github.com/goldendoglinux/support/issues) and explain your question.

Support is only given in Spanish or English at the moment.


## Connect to IRC

irc.libera.chat #goldendoglinux 

This is another way to obtain support, though volunteers might not be available at the time of your question. Please be patient for a reply.


## Documentation

Visit [https://goldendoglinux.org/docs](https://goldendoglinux.org/docs) To see if your question has already been answered in a document.

Docs are available in English and Spanish. Use the language switcher up top on the website. Default is English.
